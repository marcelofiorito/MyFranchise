# Demo Script — RunMyFranchise

[🇧🇷 Português](ROTEIRO_DEMO.md) · **🇬🇧 English**

**Dragons' Den: Learn to Win 2026 · Aug 26 · 15 min demo + 5 min Q&A · live**

> Through-line: the journey of **Alexandre Mendes** (Director of Operations, 280 stores) discovering and solving the problem of **Store 147 (Porto Alegre, score 32)**. Each app is an act. This is not a "screen tour" — it's "from pain to value in 15 minutes."

---

## Narrative arc (the "why" before the "what")

**Pain (30s opening, no screen):** "Alexandre has 280 franchisees. Each one is an island. KPIs arrive by spreadsheet, weeks late. Compliance is a manual audit. Opening a store takes months. He wants to double the network — but he can't even see the network he already has."

**Turning point:** "What if the franchisor could see the health of the entire network in real time, compliance detected itself, AI suggested the action, and the franchisee received it all spelled out on their phone?"

**Resolution:** the 4 apps, ending with the franchisee who receives the AI recommendation.

---

## Blocks (target timing: 13 min of demo + 2 of slack)

### ACT 1 — Network Panel · the view that didn't exist (≈3 min)
**App:** Network Panel (Manager)
1. Open the app. **Criticality donut** front and center: X critical / Y warning / Z healthy. "This is the entire network, right now."
2. Value line: *"Before, this was a spreadsheet consolidated at the end of the month. Now it's real time and visual."*
3. Filter/look at the table → **Store 147 in red, score 32**. Click the row.
4. Object Page for the unit: data, score, history. "This is our problem store. Let's understand why."

**Value point:** instant network visibility · prioritization by exception (the red jumps out).

### ACT 2 — Compliance · the problem explains itself (≈3 min)
**App:** Governance & Compliance (Manager)
1. Open it. Show the **deviations for 147**: Casual Sneakers −14.3% (High), Cap −24.1% (High), Dress −8.8% (Medium), unauthorized product (Mix/High).
2. Line: *"Nobody audited this by hand. The system detected the price deviation at the moment of sale"* (mention the `after CREATE` handler on VendaPraticada).
3. Show the color-coded severity (High = red). "The franchisor already knows where to turn the screw."

**Value point:** automatic and proactive compliance · from manual audit to real-time detection.

### ACT 3 — AI · from information to action (≈3 min) ⭐ climax
**App:** still in Manager / or already in the Portal
1. "Detecting the problem is half of it. The other half is: what do you do?"
2. Trigger / show the **recommendations generated by gpt-4o** (AI Core / GenAI Hub) for 147: fix pricing, restock, training.
3. Key line: *"This is not a fixed hard-coded rule. It's an LLM reading the store's real data and recommending the action — running inside our own BTP, on AI Core."*
4. (If triggering it live: have the button/route ready and tested; otherwise, show the 3 already generated.)

**Value point:** generative AI applied to the business · data → decision · runs on SAP's AI Core (governance/data in BTP).

### ACT 4 — Franchisee Portal · value reaches the front line (≈3 min)
**App:** Franchisee Portal (log in as Roberto / u147)
1. Open the OVP dashboard: 5 cards, each one "**Porto Alegre Store**".
2. **My Performance**: Jun/2026 revenue R$ 162k... in BRL, with a visible month-over-month decline.
3. **Health Score 32** in red · **Pending Actions** (color-coded severity) · **AI Recommendations** (the same as Act 3, now in the franchisee's hands).
4. Closing line: *"The franchisee doesn't need to understand BI. They open their phone and see what to do today. High = red. The whole network speaks the same language."*

**Value point:** self-service experience · same data, different roles · attribute-based security (they only see their own store).

### CLOSING (≈1 min, no screen)
- Recap the journey: visibility → cause → action (AI) → execution on the front line.
- 1 architecture line: *"All on SAP BTP: CAP + HANA Cloud + Fiori + Work Zone + AI Core. A franchisor's day of work, in a single flow."*
- Bridge to the roadmap: SAC (advanced analytics), Datasphere, Joule.

## Likely questions from the jury (Q&A — 5 min)
- "Is the AI reliable?" → it runs on AI Core (SAP governance), with a **rules fallback** if the LLM goes down — it never breaks.
- "Does it scale to 280 stores?" → HANA Cloud + in-database aggregation; the donut uses native `$apply`/aggregation.
- "Security of the franchisee's data?" → attribute-based authorization (`@restrict`), each one only sees their own unit.
- "How long to implement?" → CAP + Fiori Elements = little code, a lot through annotations; MVP in weeks.
- "Roadmap?" → SAC, Datasphere, Joule (already mentioned in the architecture).
